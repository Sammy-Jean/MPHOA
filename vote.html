The Management Vote

The board has the authority to choose a management company on its own, but this decision belongs to the whole community, so we're putting it to a vote. Review the comparison below and cast your vote: one vote per home.

View the full presentation How to vote

Why we're holding a vote

Choosing who manages our community is one of the most important decisions an HOA makes. Rather than decide it behind closed doors, the board wants every household to weigh in. Below is an honest, side-by-side look at our current company and the proposed alternative so you can decide for yourself.
