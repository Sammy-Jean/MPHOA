Architectural Review

ARC Guidelines

Clear, plain-language guidelines for what you can do without a request, and what needs board approval. Updated for our community by your HOA Board.

Our guiding principle

Maintenance items do not need an ARC request. Structural or permanent changes do. When in doubt, ask yourself: am I keeping things up, or am I changing something? Upkeep is routine. Permanent changes need approval.

What's New

A summary of recent updates to our ARC approach.

  • A limited list of common items, listed below, no longer requires an ARC request.
  • Everything else still requires an ARC request, including all structural and permanent changes.
  • The board is reviewing the ARC fee structure to better serve homeowners.

No ARC request needed (limited list)

Below is a list of common items homeowners have requested. These items, and only these items, no longer require an ARC request. Everything else still requires an ARC request.

  • Mulch (any natural wood color, including brown, black, red, and cedar)
  • Pine needles
  • Wood chips
  • Nuggets
  • Rocks
  • Borders (stone or plastic barriers for landscaping)
  • Garbage can covers, provided they are solid construction (no holes or lattice) and a solid color matching your siding or another color on the home
  • Planting flowers
  • Storm doors that match the house or door color

These changes apply only to the items listed above. All other parts of our CC&Rs and ARC guidelines still apply. If your project is not on the list, please submit an ARC request.

Important · If you received a notice or paid the fee

If you received a notice or paid the fee

If you received a notice on or after June 2nd asking you to submit an ARC request for an item on the list above, you can respectfully disregard that notification.

If you paid the $38 ARC fee on or after June 2nd for a request for an item on the list above, a credit will be issued on your next billing cycle through Kuester.

If you received a notice before June 2nd, please continue to submit the ARC request as originally instructed.

Kuester is currently updating their system to reflect these changes. For any questions in the meantime, please email the board at info@mpyork.com.

If you are unsure whether your project requires an ARC request, please reach out to the management company before starting work. We would rather answer a quick question than ask a homeowner to undo something.

How to submit an ARC request

Log in to your Kuester portal or call. You can include as many projects as you like on a single request, and we encourage it.

The review process

The management company manages the administrative side of each ARC request. They keep records, communicate with the homeowner during the process, and submit the completed request to the board for final approval. The board's role is the final review and decision.

The ARC fee on a request is the management company's charge for processing the request, not a board fee. The HOA does not keep any portion of the fee, and the board does not set it. The board is currently reviewing the fee structure to better serve homeowners.
