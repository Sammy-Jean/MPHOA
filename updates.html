Community Update

Updates to Our Enforcement Process

We have heard from many of you that our community rules are not always being followed, and that enforcement has not been as consistent as it should be. The Board listened. Based on this feedback from the community, we have adopted an updated Compliance and Hearing Policy to improve the enforcement of our Covenants, Conditions, and Restrictions. The updated policy is now in effect.

These changes are a direct response to residents who have asked us for better and more consistent enforcement. Our goal is to make sure the rules apply fairly to everyone, so that residents who are following them are not left shouldering the impact of those who are not.

What is changing

The previous courtesy notice stage has been removed from the enforcement process. The updated sequence of violation notices is:

1. Notice of Violation Letter

2. Notice of Hearing

3. Hearing Results Notice

4. Notice of Fine

Residents who receive a Notice of Violation Letter are expected to resolve the issue, typically before the next monthly inspection. If it is not resolved by then, the matter will escalate to a hearing before the Board of Directors. At the hearing, the resident has the opportunity to be heard and present information. The Board then reviews what was presented and issues a written decision, which is emailed and mailed to the homeowner.

Important clarification: A Notice of Violation Letter is not a fine. A fine can only be assessed after a hearing has been held and the Board has determined, in its written decision, that a fine is warranted. If you have questions about this process or a specific concern on your property, please reach out to Kuester.

Areas of focus for increased enforcement

The Board will be increasing enforcement in several areas commonly cited by residents in community feedback. We encourage all homeowners to review their property and address any issues to avoid receiving a violation notice.

Landscaping maintenance: Lawns kept neat and mowed, weeds removed, dead plants replaced, and planting beds maintained.

Algae growth on homes: Siding, roofing, and other exterior surfaces that show algae or mildew growth should be pressure washed and cleaned.

Parking: Street parking is generally prohibited by our CC&Rs. Vehicles should be parked in garages or driveways, with limited exceptions for oversized vehicles during daylight hours.

Trail Paving Feedback

Thank You for Your Input

The community feedback period for the proposed trail paving project closed on October 17. We received 41 responses from residents, which the Board truly appreciates.

Support the proposal: 25 responses

Do not support proceeding at this time: 12 responses

Undecided: 4 responses

The responses included thoughtful comments covering a wide range of perspectives, questions, and alternative ideas. The Board is reviewing all of the input, including the comments submitted alongside each response.

Next steps and the Board's plan for the trail paving project will be discussed at our upcoming Annual Meeting.
