Community Proposal

Trail Paving Proposal - We Want Your Feedback

The Board is considering a proposal to pave two community trails this year: the Canoga path and the Olympia path. A third path, the Smith Cliffs path, would follow in Q3 of 2027. This makes it a two-year plan spread across two budget cycles.

The total estimated cost for all three paths is approximately $98,545, funded entirely from Association reserves. Monthly dues will not increase as a result of this decision. The Association has strong reserves that make this possible without impacting homeowner assessments.

This would be the first major change to Monterey Park and the first capital investment made by the Association since the community was established. Because of the significance of this decision, the Board wants to hear from residents before moving forward.

Proposed trail locations: Canoga (red), Olympia (blue), Smith Cliffs (purple, Q3 2027)

The Board is asking the community for input. Residents can share their support for:

  • Moving forward with paving the Canoga and Olympia paths this year, with the Smith Cliffs path to follow in Q3 of 2027
  • Not proceeding with paving at this time

Your feedback will help the Board decide whether to proceed.

Please share your feedback by Saturday, October 17, 2026. After comments have been reviewed, the Board will determine whether to proceed and will set an installation date based on the community's input.
