Community Update

Updates to Our Enforcement Process

We have heard from many of you that our community rules are not always being followed, and that enforcement has not been as consistent as it should be. The Board listened. Based on this feedback from the community, we have adopted an updated Compliance and Hearing Policy to improve the enforcement of our Covenants, Conditions, and Restrictions. The updated policy was adopted on October 6, 2026 and is now in effect.

These changes are a direct response to residents who have asked us for better and more consistent enforcement. Our goal is to make sure the rules apply fairly to everyone, so that residents who are following them are not left shouldering the impact of those who are not.

What is changing

The previous courtesy notice stage has been removed from the enforcement process. The updated sequence of violation notices is:

1. Notice of Violation Letter

2. Notice of Hearing

3. Hearing Results Notice

4. Notice of Fine

Residents receiving a Notice of Violation Letter will be given a specified period of time to resolve the issue. If the violation is not cured and no response is received within that time, the matter will escalate to a hearing.

Hearings before the Board of Directors give residents the opportunity to be heard and to present information regarding the charges. The Board does not make a decision at the hearing itself. After the hearing, the Board reviews the information presented and then issues a written decision, which is emailed and mailed to the homeowner.

Important clarification: Fines are not issued automatically. A fine can only be assessed after a Notice of Violation Letter has been issued, a hearing has been held, and the Board of Directors has issued a written decision determining that a fine is warranted. Every resident has the opportunity to resolve the issue, respond to the notice, and be heard at the hearing before any fine is applied.

We have heard rumors in the community suggesting that residents are being fined. In some cases, residents have received a Notice of Violation Letter and may believe that is a fine. A Notice of Violation Letter is not a fine. It is a written notice that an issue has been identified and provides a period of time to resolve it. Please disregard any rumors to the contrary, and reach out to Kuester with any questions about this process or a specific concern on your property.

Areas of focus for increased enforcement

The Board will be increasing enforcement in several areas commonly cited by residents in community feedback. We encourage all homeowners to review their property and address any issues to avoid receiving a violation notice.

Landscaping maintenance: Lawns kept neat and mowed, weeds removed, dead plants replaced, and planting beds maintained.

Algae growth on homes: Siding, roofing, and other exterior surfaces that show algae or mildew growth should be pressure washed and cleaned.

Parking: Street parking is generally prohibited by our CC&Rs. Vehicles should be parked in garages or driveways, with limited exceptions for oversized vehicles during daylight hours.
