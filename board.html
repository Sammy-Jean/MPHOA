Street parking is meant for short-term, temporary use, such as having a guest over for dinner, a family gathering, a contractor doing work at your home, or a delivery. It is not meant as a daily parking space, and overnight street parking is not permitted under our community's CC&Rs.

The reasons are practical: keeping streets clear helps emergency vehicles, trash and delivery trucks access every home easily, and it keeps sightlines clear at corners and driveways. Driveways and garages are intended to be the primary parking for each home.

Under our current management company, each ARC application carries a $38 fee paid by the homeowner. We want to be clear that the HOA keeps none of this fee and the board does not set it; it is the management company's charge, and it passes through us to them. Separately, the board has updated our ARC guidelines. A limited list of common items no longer requires an ARC request, including mulch, pine needles, wood chips, nuggets, rocks, borders, garbage can covers, planting flowers, and storm doors that match the house. Everything else still requires an ARC request. See the full list and what to do if you received a recent notice at mpyork.com/arc.

A reminder is not a fine. The violation process has five stages, and you get four chances to address an issue before a fine is ever issued.

  • Stage 1 — Friendly Reminder: First notice of an issue. No fine. Just an FYI to give you a chance to address it.
  • Stage 2 — Warning: Formal notice that the issue still exists. Still no fine.
  • Stage 3 — Notice of Hearing: Invitation to a hearing where you can present your side before any decision is made.
  • Stage 4 — Notice of Hearing Results: Outcome of the hearing.
  • Stage 5 — Notice of Fine: Only at this stage is a fine actually issued.

Our goal at every stage is to work with homeowners, not against them. If you receive a notice and have questions, please reach out to the management company or email us at info@mpyork.com.
