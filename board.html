Submitted Questions & Answers

These are questions homeowners submitted ahead of the meeting, with the board's answers. Tap any question to read the answer.

At $82 a month, Monterey Park has the lowest dues of comparable York-area communities. Your dues cover landscaping and grounds, the management contract, insurance, the pond, common areas, and contributions to reserves. Every dollar is in the financial statements, which any homeowner can review.

Grounds is our largest category at about $81,000 budgeted for the year. The base grass-cutting contract is about $28,080. The rest covers seasonal work like mulch and plantings, the pond, pet-waste stations, and repairs such as trees and signage as needs come up. Every part is itemized and accounted for.

This is fair feedback. Maintaining common areas is part of what your dues pay for, and where work has fallen short, that is on us to correct with our grounds vendor. If you see something that needs attention, report it at mpyork.com and it goes straight to the board.

Parking is governed by our community rules. We are reviewing the guidance so everyone gets one consistent answer, and we will publish it through our official channels.

Safety matters to all of us. Public streets are handled by local law enforcement, and we encourage residents to report unsafe driving. The board is also exploring options the community can weigh in on.

You can include multiple projects on a single ARC request. Just describe each clearly so the review covers everything you are planning.

Submit it through the management portal at Kuester.com, call (704) 973-9019, or email support@kuester.com. Reports are handled confidentially and the property is inspected.

As of April 30, about $15,300 was outstanding across roughly 28 accounts. This is not lost money. We follow an active collections process with late fees and liens, applied consistently and fairly to everyone.

If you have fallen behind, please reach out to us or the management company. We would rather work with you than against you, and we will help you find a path forward.

We welcome ideas. Some have real costs the whole community would share, so they are decisions for the community, not just the board. Bring them to a meeting and we will look at them honestly together.

Under our current management company, each ARC application carries a $38 fee paid by the homeowner. This is one reason we are putting the management question to a community vote. The proposed alternative would have the board review requests directly, with no ARC fee to homeowners.

The Management Vote

The board has the authority to choose a management company on its own, but this decision belongs to the whole community, so we're putting it to a vote: Kuester (our current company) or Red Rock (proposed). One vote per home.
