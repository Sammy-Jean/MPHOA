A Note from the Board

Thank you, Tracy

A board update and what comes next for our community.

We want to share with the community that Tracy Blech has decided to step down from the board for personal and health reasons.

Tracy has served thoughtfully since the homeowner-led board began in January. She contributed time, energy, and care to the work of standing up our community's first resident-led governance. We are deeply grateful for everything she gave to this community, and we will miss her on the board.

We respect her decision fully, and we ask the community to do the same. Please join us in wishing Tracy and her family well.

Tracy, thank you. For your service, your thoughtfulness, and your care. We wish you and your family the very best.

Respect for those who serve

Volunteer board members give their time to serve this community. They deserve to be able to do that work with the kind of respect that any neighbor would extend to another. That standard matters not just for the people currently serving, but for everyone who may serve in the future.

What Comes Next

Filling the open board seat

Our bylaws, Article V Section 4(b), provide that vacancies caused by resignation are filled by majority vote of the remaining members of the board. Our bylaws do not permit an official membership vote to fill a vacancy during a board member's term. While we are not required to do this, we believe it is a good opportunity to promote transparency and encourage community involvement in the process.

Over the next several weeks, the board will follow this process:

July 1 - 15
Application window. Neighbors who are interested in serving are invited to apply.
July 16 - 22
Interviews. The board will reach out to applicants by email.
July 23 - 24
Finalists selected based on our criteria.
July 25 - Aug 1
Informal, non-binding community vote on the finalists.
August 5
Appointment announced.

We will share details on how to apply in a separate message in the coming days. We will continue to update the community at each stage of the process.

As always, you can reach the board at any time at info@mpyork.com.

With gratitude,
The Board
