Our Community Standard

Respect for those who serve

Volunteer board members give their time to serve this community. They deserve to be able to do that work with the kind of respect that any neighbor would extend to another. That standard matters not just for the people currently serving, but for everyone who may serve in the future.

What Comes Next

Filling the open board seat

Our bylaws, Article V Section 4(b), provide that vacancies caused by resignation are filled by majority vote of the remaining members of the board. Our bylaws do not permit an official membership vote to fill a vacancy during a board member's term. While we are not required to do this, we believe it is a good opportunity to promote transparency and encourage community involvement in the process.

Over the next several weeks, the board will:

  • Open a two-week window for neighbors to apply (July 1 - July 15)
  • Conduct interviews with applicants (July 16 - July 22)
  • Identify finalists based on our criteria (July 23 - July 24)
  • Hold an informal, non-binding community vote on the finalists (July 25 - August 1)
  • Make the appointment and announce our decision (August 5)

We will share details on how to apply in a separate message in the coming days. We will continue to update the community at each stage of the process.

As always, you can reach the board at any time at info@mpyork.com.
