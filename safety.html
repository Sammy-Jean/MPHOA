Staying Aware and Safe

Simple habits to help keep our community, our cars, and our homes secure.

We wanted to share a brief safety reminder with the community. There have been reports of attempted and actual break-ins in and around our area recently, including car break-ins. We think it is worth a note to encourage everyone to stay aware.

This is not a cause for alarm. Most property crimes are preventable with simple habits.

Stay Informed

For current crime statistics and reports for York County, visit the York County Sheriff's Office annual report and crime information page at yorkcountysheriff.com.

Simple Habits That Help

  • Lock your car.Every time. Most vehicle break-ins target unlocked cars.
  • Do not leave valuables visible.Wallets, laptops, purses, and shopping bags in plain sight invite trouble. Store them in the trunk or take them inside.
  • Do not leave keys or garage door openers in your car.A garage door opener in an unlocked car can give a thief access to your home.
  • Park in your driveway or garage when possible.Vehicles parked on the street are more likely to be targeted. Keeping cars off the street when you can helps reduce that risk.
  • Close and lock the garage door.Even when you are home. An open garage is an invitation.
  • Turn on exterior lights at night.Well-lit driveways and porches are less attractive to would-be thieves.
  • Look out for your neighbors.If you see something that seems off, do not confront it. Note what you can (vehicle, direction, description) and report it to the York County Sheriff's Office or call 911.

Where to Report and Stay Informed

Property crime is a law enforcement matter, and reports should go directly to the York County Sheriff's Office, not the HOA.
