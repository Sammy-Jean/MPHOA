Welcome to Monterey Park

Whether you just moved in or you have been here for years, here is what you need to know about your community, how things work, and where to find what you need.

The 30-second orientation

Monterey Park is a 157-home community in York, South Carolina, governed by a volunteer board of homeowners.

Day-to-day operations (payments, work orders, ARC requests, notices) are handled by our management company, Kuester. The board sets direction, makes decisions, and answers to the community.

Most questions are answered on this page. Anything you cannot find here, please email info@mpyork.com and we will get back to you.

The basics

The most common things you will need:

Pay HOA dues

Dues are paid through Kuester. Log in at kuester.com or call (704) 973-9019.

Submit a work request

For broken lights, potholes, damaged signs, or other common-area issues. Submit through Kuester. No fee.

Contact the board

Email info@mpyork.com. We aim to respond within 2-3 business days.

Governing documents

The CC&Rs, Bylaws, and Architectural Guidelines are available through the Kuester portal.

The most common questions

If you only read one section, read this one. These are the things most often asked.

Trash day - Mondays

Containers can go out no earlier than 5:00 PM the day before pickup and must be brought back in by 9:00 PM on pickup day. At all other times they need to be stored out of sight. The trash service provider is selected by the Association.

Parking

Park in your garage or your own driveway. Street parking within the community is generally prohibited. Vehicles too large for a driveway may park on the street directly in front of the owner's lot during daylight hours only. No vehicle may block a sidewalk.

Boats, campers, RVs, ATVs, trailers, tractors, and mowers must be kept in an enclosed garage or screened out of sight.

Pets

Dogs, cats, and other common household pets are welcome for personal (non-commercial) use, with a limit of no more than 3 dogs, 3 cats, and/or 3 of any other household pet (excluding fish). Pets must be leashed on Association property at all times, and owners must clean up after them.

Holiday decorations

Temporary holiday decorations are permitted October 1 through January 31. For other holidays, decorations may go up one week before and must come down one week after.

Mailbox and trash can covers

Garbage can covers do not require an ARC request, provided they are solid construction (no holes or lattice) and a solid color matching your siding or another color on the home.

Signs

Most signs need prior written approval. The main exception is one "for sale" sign no larger than 30" x 24".
